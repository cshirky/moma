Chronology Challenge

Put The Museum of Modern Art’s paintings in the right order

Five stages, each one harder.

You’ll be shown paintings currently on display at MoMA, but displayed out of chronological order. Drag them so the most recent painting is on top and the oldest is on the bottom. Click any painting to see a larger version.

You will not need to guess the year, just which painting was created before which.
