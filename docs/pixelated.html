Pixelated

Name the painting from as few squares of colour as you can

Each painting starts as four squares of colour, each the average of that quarter of the canvas. Click it to split it into finer squares: 3×3, then 4×4, up to 10×10, then the painting itself. Every click costs a point. Drag each label onto the painting it belongs to.

Images and data from The Museum of Modern Art’s public collection dataset.

Paintings shown are on view at MoMA as of the most recent data refresh.
